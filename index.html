Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Vardiveer, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Developer Information

This application is developed and operated by Vardiveer. For any privacy-related concerns, you can contact us at support@vardiveer.com.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with a personalized preparation experience, we collect the following data:

  • Personal Information: Full Name, Email Address, and Mobile Number.
  • Authentication Data: Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • App Activity: Daily test history, practice test results, and bookmarked questions.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and maintain your student account.
  • To track your subject-wise progress and generate performance analytics.
  • To send important transactional emails (like OTPs for registration or password resets).

4. Data Security & Third-Party Services

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (a leading enterprise-grade database provider). All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS. We use Brevo for secure email delivery. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Data Retention Policy

We retain your personal data only for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will permanently erase your profile, test history, and all associated data from our active databases within 30 days. Residual backup copies may be retained for up to 90 days for disaster recovery purposes, after which they are also permanently deleted.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete such information.

Account Deletion Request

You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

You can delete your account directly from the Profile Section inside the app. If you are unable to access the app, you can request deletion via our secure web portal below.

Proceed to Account Deletion Portal

Warning: Once deleted, your test history, practice results, and profile data cannot be recovered. Requests are processed within 24-48 hours.
